# BEYİN-KÖKÜ / BRAINROOT — screenplay

The contract between the words (`src/stories/beyinkoku/text.ts`,
`timeline.ts`) and the set (`src/three/beyinkoku/`). Every segment below says
what the image shows while each line is on screen. Positions are in meters;
tune lights and cameras freely as long as the relationships marked **(must)**
still read.

- 96 segments · 12 chapters · **172.1 screens** · `SCREEN_VH = 95`
- 2 choices (the TV remote; the two floor lines) · 10 discoveries
- Six "Üç saniye." cuts (five to the real lab, one on Veyl's face)
- Rail: right — keep the subject of every frame left of centre.

---

## 1. Logline, tone, palette, light

**Logline.** Professor Veyl keeps hundreds of bodies wired to small personal
paradises built from their own memories. One of them, Levy A., an archive
clerk in a perfect suburb, has begun to notice that his reasons arrive half a
second after his acts. Veyl calls him in — slowly — tears the veil, explains,
and weaves it again.

**Tone.** Clinical, quiet, uncanny; the horror is in the gap between a gesture
and its reason. The suburb is too soft, the Centre too clean, the real lab too
cold. Violence is implied by metal, cables, light and sound, never by tissue.
The camera is calm and symmetrical in the simulation, handheld only on the
second floor, and locked-off in the real lab.

## 2. World layout

Axes: x right, y up, z toward the default camera (people mostly face −z).
Every set is far from the others; cuts are camera jumps. All simulation sets
(B–H) share one design rule: **no sharp corners** — every table, sill, frame
and counter has a visible bevel (radius 3–8 cm). The real lab (A) is the only
place with hard edges.

## 6. The devices (for the builder)

**"Üç saniye." — the cut to the real lab.** Five times (`lab1`, `lab2`,
`lab3`, `lab4`, `lab5`) the same grammar, so the reader learns it:

1. Hard cut to two frames of white; the centre word "Üç saniye." appears
   over the white and fades as the image resolves (≈ 0.4 screens, always
   u 0.02 → ≈ 0.2 of the segment).
2. The flash set: station 31 under the surgical lamp (`lab1`–`lab3`), or the
   control desk with the chair beyond the glass (`lab4`, `lab5`) —
   overexposed cold white, bloom, desaturated, everything outside the lamp
   black. Locked-off camera or one slow lateral move. Sound drops to a
   monitor beep and the lamp's buzz.
   The rail keeps the same rhythm every time: a place line ("Başka bir
   yer." → "Gerçek laboratuvar." / "Gerçek oda."), then the voice, then the
   next segment opens with "Sonra yeniden koridor / oda / sedye." on the cut
   back.
3. Never closer than 1.2 m to the head; the halo always hides the cut (a dark
   line under its edge at most); no tissue, no blood, no open skull ever
   modelled. Faces of the real Levy are never shown frontally.
4. Hard cut back (two frames of white again), sound returns mid-phrase.

The fifth "Üç saniye." (`face`, between `lab4` and `lab5`) breaks the pattern on purpose: black instead
of white, a typed line instead of the lab, and `panic` opens with
"Sonra yine yorgun, yaşlı bir adamdı." as the way back.

**The monster distortion** (second floor; Veyl's face once). Brief, never a
lingering close-up:

- Face: vertex stretch along y (×1.3–1.4) with the jaw dropping; eyes become
  flat black discs; along the ring-scar line a thin black band opens (a
  dark emissive ring), with a faint wet grey specular gleam inside — no
  modelled tissue.
- Light carries it: fluorescents stutter; the key flips to a low under-light
  (green-white) that throws tall stretched shadows up the walls; between
  flickers the faces snap back to normal.
- Devices: the 0.14 m pen extrudes into a 0.6 m thin black cone (needle);
  in `swarm`, hooks (a bent thin torus segment) and short pipes.
- Overlap in `swarm`: duplicate the face meshes with 30 % opacity and a
  slight offset so faces "blur into each other".
- Voices: dry and human on the first syllable, then pitched down and torn.

**The veil re-woven** (`goodday`): a fine woven-thread texture scanline
passing down the frame; the image after it is a touch warmer and softer.

## 9. Notes for the builder

1. Keep subjects left of centre: the rail is on the right.
2. World texts are the reader's only copy of the long documents (file card,
   protocol, notice, boards, log, result, entry page): frame them large and
   flat to the camera, high contrast, and hold them still while they are up.
3. Repetition is a device: `sameLight` = `mirror` u 0.45, `again` = `elen`,
   `cantina`/`window` = `why`/`wave`, `next` u 0.35 = `slower` u 0.34. Use the
   identical camera, light and poses so the reader feels the loop.
4. The simulation never has sharp corners or hard shadows; the real lab
   never has anything soft.
5. Everyone in the simulation has the ring scar; the real Veyl does not.
6. The red line is the story's thread: from the Centre floor to the stairs,
   along the second-floor corridor, under the last door, into the dark — and
   in `cover` and `log` as a memory.
7. Restraint: no modelled brain tissue, no open skull, no blood except the
   colour pulse in `layer`; the needle goes in off-frame.
